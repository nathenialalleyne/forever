## Question

Can the pinned Matcha baseline deliver an inspectable instruction path using one recipe
viewer and one contextual information tool, without a pile of disconnected guidebooks,
and what does a unified Field Journal actually need to do?

## Advancement noise: measured, and much smaller than feared

Matcha ships **246 advancement files**, which sounds alarming for a pack that has taken a
position against advancement spam. The composition matters more than the count.

| Section | Files | Character |
|---|---:|---|
| `recipe_unlocks` | 141 | Plumbing. Unlocks a recipe in the book; not a player-facing milestone. |
| `tutorial` | 59 | The onboarding path. |
| `mechanics` | 20 | Systems explanation. |
| `hell` | 8 | Nether progression. |
| `end` | 5 | End progression. |
| other | 13 | Cooking recipes, particles, multiplayer support. |

**Only 69 of 243 have `show_toast` enabled.** Matcha has already suppressed the toast and
chat announcement on 174 of them. Of the 69 a player actually sees, 58 are in `tutorial`,
7 in `hell`, and 4 in `end`.

Only 3 root advancements exist, so Matcha contributes **three tabs**, not one tab per
subsystem. That is the specific failure mode
[ADR 0029](../../docs/adr/0029-unified-contextual-knowledge.md) warns about, and Matcha
does not commit it.

**Classification against the ticket's three categories:**

- *Required instruction*: the 59 `tutorial` and 20 `mechanics` entries. These carry the
  process text a player needs and must not be suppressed.
- *Useful discovery*: `hell` and `end` roots and their 11 visible children.
- *Redundant notification*: none found. The 141 recipe unlocks would be redundant if they
  toasted, and Matcha already silences them.

**Proposed configuration: change nothing.** The pack should not suppress Matcha
advancements. There is no measured noise problem to solve, and every suppression risks
removing instruction, which the ticket explicitly forbids.

## Guidebook duplication: none exists

This was the largest expected risk and it did not materialise.

- Matcha grants **no starter book**. Its only first-join style function is
  `first_dragon_killed_reward.mcfunction`, which is a progression reward, not an
  onboarding book.
- Matcha ships no Patchouli or guidebook assets. The seven files matching `book` are a
  bookshelf model, bookshelf recipes and loot, and a `hell_bound_book` game item.
- **Jade ships zero guidebook assets.**
- REI matched `book` twice, but both are `ManualFilteringRule` classes, an internal
  filtering feature. Not a guidebook.

So the "shelf of manuals" problem does not exist on the current baseline. It becomes a
risk only when a content mod that ships its own book is adopted, which is a constraint on
future lab decisions rather than a problem to fix now.

Matcha's instruction channel is the advancement description. Every visible advancement has
translated instruction text: 140 `adv.kleispack.*` keys, of which **69 are descriptions,
exactly matching the 69 visible advancements**. Sample:

```
adv.kleispack.cook_food       = "Powerful Flavours"
adv.kleispack.cook_food.desc  = "Reveal the hidden aspect of a Food by cooking it on a
                                 Campfire or an Oven"
```

That is a real instruction, not a flavour line, and it satisfies design principle 2
without any pack work.

## Missing-viewer fallback: verified

Removing REI, Jade, Architectury, and Cloth Config and booting a fresh world produced
identical results: `Loaded 2346 recipes`, `Loaded 1805 advancements`, `Done (1.700s)`, and
Matcha still enabled in `level.dat`.

Ordinary play and the instruction path survive without any viewer, because the instruction
lives in Matcha's advancements rather than in a mod-provided book. Acceptance criterion 6
is satisfied.

## Field Journal requirements

The ticket asks what a unified journal must do. Based on what was observed:

1. **Contextual entry points.** Jade's `IBlockComponentProvider` and
   `IComponentProvider` interfaces are the seam for adding a "the journal knows about
   this" line to the block tooltip, without the journal owning the HUD.
2. **Recipe links.** REI exposes `me.shedaniel.rei.api.client.registry` and
   `...api.client.plugins`. A journal entry should be able to open the viewer at a
   specific recipe rather than describing it in prose.
3. **Matcha normalisation.** Journal entries must reference Matcha concepts through the
   existing adapter, never by hardcoding `adv.kleispack.*` keys or `data/main/...` paths.
   ADR 0002 and principle 13 already require this.
4. **Source metadata.** An entry derived from a Matcha advancement should record that
   provenance, so a Matcha update that removes an advancement produces a visible stale
   entry rather than a silent lie.
5. **Discovery state.** The four states from the brief, Rumored, Observed, Understood,
   Mastered, need somewhere to live. Matcha advancement completion is a usable input
   signal for Observed and Understood.
6. **Failure reasons.** When something cannot be done, the journal should say why, which
   is the same described-result requirement recorded in ADR 0022.
7. **Optional-provider fallback.** Since ordinary play survives without REI and Jade, the
   journal must degrade to text when either is absent. It must not hard-depend on a
   client-only viewer.

**No custom code is justified by this lab.** The custom-code gate in the ticket is not
met: the instruction path already works through Matcha's own advancements, and the two
viewers integrate without pack code. A journal remains desirable for *unifying* knowledge
across future mods, but M2 should not begin until a lab shows a real information gap.

## Performance observations

Server startup was `1.478s` with the information stack and `1.700s` without it, on an
AMD Ryzen 7 9700X with 3 GB allocated. The difference is within run-to-run noise and no
conclusion should be drawn from it. Client-side rendering cost is untested.

## Conflicts observed

None. No duplicate recipe, duplicate item, competing guidebook, or competing advancement
tree was found.

One dependency observation: Jade lists JEI as an **optional** dependency. JEI has a 26.2
release, so a future mod could pull it in and produce two recipe viewers. The pack should
explicitly exclude JEI while REI is the selected viewer, because two viewers is precisely
the duplication this lab exists to prevent.

## Does it fit the pack?

Yes, for both candidates, with the client verification still outstanding.
